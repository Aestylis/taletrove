Questions

Fair questions.

Is it really free, or is this a trial?

Yes. Everything is free, forever. TaleTrove is a passion project. If it helps your table, buy the developer a coffee on Ko-fi.

Where is my world stored?

On your device, in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Export a .trv backup anytime and sync it to your own Google Drive if you like.

Do my players need accounts?

No. Nobody needs an account, including you. Export a player bundle with the secrets stripped out. Your players open it in a browser.

How is this different from LegendKeeper or World Anvil?

Those are server-hosted platforms with subscriptions. TaleTrove is local-first and free. Your world works offline and never expires with a plan.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Open taletrove.app in your phone's browser and add it to your home screen. It installs like an app from there, no app store needed. It works offline once installed.
